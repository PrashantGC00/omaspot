Spotify Overlay

A drop-down terminal you open with Super Shift M to search songs, open your playlists and control playback. The terminal only controls Spotify. Audio plays through Spotify Soloist, the official background player, so the librespot key block on your account doesn't apply.

The mockup opens in focus mode. Its controls sit over the art: shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat, the seek bar and a volume slider you can drag. exit focus (or esc) goes back to the normal layout with its player bar. Buttons use Nerd Font icons from JetBrainsMono Nerd Font, which your terminals already use. Example songs and playlists, not your library; the cover and album colours are illustrative. vinyl is a terminal program laid out like a desktop music app: a title bar, a sidebar, the main pane and a player bar along the bottom. It works with the mouse and with keys (/ search, enter play, space play/pause, « » previous/next, f focus, esc leave focus or hide).

  

    
Focus mode

    
Focus mode fills vinyl's window with the song playing. At the back, the audio visualizer (24 columns of ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█ from cava) is translucent and blurred. In front of it sits the song cover, and in the centre the record spins at 33⅓ rpm, translucent and slightly blurred, drawn in text symbols: ░ ▒ ▓ █ for the record and its label, ◉ │ ╯ ╭ ■ for the tonearm, which lifts when you pause. The window itself turns see-through, so your wallpaper shows faintly behind it. The bottom player bar hides and its controls move into the view: title and artist on the left, shuffle, previous, play/pause, next and repeat with the seek bar in the middle, and the volume slider on the right.

  






  
How the pieces connect

  
Three parts, each doing one job. The overlay is just a terminal window Hyprland shows and hides. Our terminal app, vinyl, talks to Spotify's official Web API for playlists, search and remote control. Soloist sits in the background as a Spotify Connect speaker named "Omarchy" and plays the audio.

FunctionPartWhat it does

    
      
spotify-overlay toggleoursShows the overlay if hidden, hides it if visible. Starts it first if it isn't running.

      
spotify-overlay ensureRunningoursOpens a terminal with class spotify-overlay running vinyl, and checks Soloist is up.

      
hide on Esc or focus lossoursMoves the window to a hidden special workspace instead of closing it, so it reopens instantly with your place kept.

      
FocusMode.enter()oursFills the window with the song, in three layers: the cava visualizer translucent and blurred at the back, the song cover in the middle, and the spinning record in the centre, translucent and slightly blurred. f, the sidebar or the focus icon in the player bar opens it; esc leaves it.

      
FocusMode.spin(playing)oursRedraws the record at 33⅓ rpm as 26×13 text symbols in the terminal font: ░ ▒ for the grooves, ▓ for the turning highlight, █ in the album colours for the label, ◉ │ ╯ ╭ ■ for the tonearm. On pause it slows to a stop and lifts the tonearm.

      
FocusMode.drawBars(levels)oursDraws 24 columns of ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█ blocks, five rows high, from cava's audio levels, in hard steps of the album's four colours, with peak caps that drop slowly. Bars sink to zero when nothing plays.

      
Palette.extract(cover)oursDownloads the cover once, picks its four main colours, and caches them per album. The cover itself becomes the record's label.

      
NowPlaying.onTrackChangeoursNotices a new song and sends its title, cover and colours to focus mode and the player bar, which fade to the new colours.

      
ThemeSyncoursRuns on omarchy theme set and rewrites vinyl's colours from the new theme.

      
listPlaylists / openPlaylistoursYour own and followed playlists, Liked Songs, saved albums and followed artists.

      
searchoursA top result, then songs, albums and playlists, like a desktop app's search page.

      
play / addToQueueoursTells Spotify to play or queue on the "Omarchy" device.

      
start / updatesoloistdKeeps Soloist running as a user service and fetches a new build when the old one expires.

Open questions

  
    
    
  • Client ID. vinyl needs a Spotify Developer app's Client ID. Yours was removed with the old plugin, so either paste it again or create a new app.
    • 
    
  • Soloist API key. soloistd asks for one. Spotify's announcement doesn't say how to get it. I'll read their getting-started guide before installing anything.
    • 
    
  • Spotify-made playlists. Newer developer apps can't read Spotify's own playlists, such as Discover Weekly and Daily Mix. Your playlists, ones you follow from other people, Liked Songs and search all work.
    • 
    
  • Bar B widget. It's not yet known whether Soloist reports what's playing to the desktop (MPRIS). If it doesn't, the bar won't show the song, but the overlay still does.
    • 
    
  • Now-playing source for focus mode. Focus mode needs the current song and cover. Plan A has vinyl share the current song with the deck. Plan B uses Soloist's own local events, if they include the cover. Both get checked at build time.
    • 
    
  • Blur in a terminal. A terminal can't blur its own text. The cover can be shown as a real image with the kitty graphics protocol (Ghostty and kitty support it, Alacritty doesn't), blurred beforehand with ImageMagick. The window's see-through background comes from the terminal's opacity plus Hyprland's blur. The blur on the visualizer and record would be faked with dim colours and soft ░▒ edges, unless we draw the whole focus view as an image each frame, which costs more CPU.
    • 
    
  • Bars need audio on this PC. cava listens to your speakers' output, so the bars move only when Soloist plays here. If you play on your phone instead, the record still spins but the bars stay flat.
    • 
    
  • Key change. Super Shift M currently opens the Spotify desktop app. The overlay would take it over. The desktop app stays installed as a fallback.
    • 
    
  • New packages. spotify-soloist-bin appeared on the AUR 16 hours ago and soloistd has 5 GitHub stars. I'd review both before installing.
    •
